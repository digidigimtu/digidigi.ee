Digidigi MTÜ

Cybersecurity awareness & capability for society

About Us

Digidigi MTÜ is an Estonian non-profit association dedicated to promoting cybersecurity and information security awareness and capability in society — including the development of security behaviour and culture within organisations and among individuals.

Founded in January 2026 and registered in Tartu, Estonia (registry code 80666302), we bring together experts, educators and organisations committed to a more secure digital society.

What We Do

  • 🎓
    Training & Workshops

    Cybersecurity and information security training, seminars, workshops and information days.

  • 🔍
    Consultations & Audits

    Risk assessment and mitigation consultations and audits for organisations and individuals.

  • 🤝
    Events & Conferences

    Conferences, forums and public events connecting field experts and interested parties.

  • 📚
    Materials & Guidelines

    Developing and distributing learning materials, guidelines and best practices.

  • 🌐
    Cooperation

    Working with government bodies, educational institutions and companies in Estonia and internationally.

  • ⚖️
    Policy & Advocacy

    Participating in legislation and policy-making as an expert and advocate.
